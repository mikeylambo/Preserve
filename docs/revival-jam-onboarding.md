# Revival Jam / Preserve: Onboarding

Companion to `revival-jam.md`. Every rule is taught by play, one at a time, **with no text at all** if playtests allow (rule 8). The only words in the first hour are the control glyphs below, shown once and only for the device in use. Teaching happens in the real first rooms, not a separate tutorial.

## 0. Boot to first control (under 20 seconds)

- Title: the specimen jar glowing teal in the dark, with the working title. **Begin** (or any button) starts.
- In-engine: a crack runs up the jar, the lid pops, and Pip slumps out onto the floor. The camera settles on room 0.01. No cutscene skip is needed because it's 6 seconds long and plays only once.
- The intake desk sits behind the jar in the room: a sample label with handwriting nobody can read and a faded photo of a sunny farmhouse kitchen. The camera lingers on the photo for one second the first time. **This is the setup for the ending; never explain it.**

## 1. Room 0.01: move and jump (par 0)

- Control glyphs fade in under Pip: move, then jump (keyboard, gamepad or touch, whichever was used last). They fade out after the first jump.
- The exit jar glows in plain sight. Entering it plays a soft glass chime and a cork pop. This teaches "the jar is the goal".

## 2. Room 0.02: you can't stay dead (par 1)

- The only way on is down a tall shaft. Pip falls, splats and respawns at the top. The step at the bottom pulses once as it forms.
- **No text.** The lesson is the step that's still there. The camera holds on it for the full respawn delay the first time.
- The scorecard appears for the first time: deaths 1, par 1, **Par**. The golf name is the only word on it.

## 3. Room 0.03: choose where (par 1)

- Two shafts. The short one is safe; only the long one splats. Dying in the short shaft is impossible, so the player learns that the hazard decides.

## 4. Room 0.04: build on your deaths (par 2)

- A ledge just out of reach above the foot of the long shaft. Two splats, stacked. The second step forming on the first is the "oh" moment of the whole game.

## 5. Room 1.01: a different death, a different material (par 1)

- The first spike field. Pip dies in it and the spikes are covered by a teal coat that looks unmistakably walkable (art bible §5).
- **Retry glyph** appears once in the corner after the first death in Sublevel 1, then never again unprompted.

## 6. Rooms 1.02 to 1.12: the rest of the sublevel

| Beat | Room | How it's taught |
|---|---|---|
| Ceilings limit where you can die | 1.02 | Visible, low ceiling; a bonk sound on the first hit |
| Coats work on walls and ceilings | 1.03, 1.07 | The spikes face the player at eye height; first death there makes a ledge |
| Combining deaths | 1.04, 1.06 | A splat lands on spikes and makes a coat: the "two rules at once" beat |
| Seeds | 1.05 | The seed glints in a pocket over spikes; touching it plays a bright pluck and adds a seed pip to the corner |
| Birdies | 1.08 | The first time a player beats par, the scorecard word changes and plays a brighter sting. No explanation |
| Test Chambers | 1.12 | The ceiling plate begins lowering on first entry, with a deep grind; the urgency is visual, never a timer number |

## 7. Introduced later, one beat each

| Thing | First room | How |
|---|---|---|
| Crust breaks once you leave it | 2.01 | A short fire strip: cross on crust, it crumbles behind you with a crackle you can't miss |
| Fill | 2.02 | A tunnel too low to jump in (prototype level 2) |
| Pads | 3.01 | The press is the only route up; the pad bounces you the moment you step on it |
| Frost freezes stillness | 4.01 | Pip visibly frosts over while standing still; moving clears it. The glyph-free lesson is that stopping is the choice |
| Splitting | 5.01 | A cutter beam right at the entry splits you; the **swap** glyph appears once, and the idle half's seed-eyes glance toward the active one |
| Strands | 6.01 | A dead door with a wire gap beside it; touching the gap kills Pip, the strand forms, and the door opens |
| Interactions | 4.05 onward | Each interaction gets one small room where it's the only thing happening |

## 9. Assists (settings, never shame)

- **Par ghost:** after 10 retries in one room, an optional faint teal ghost can show the intended solution once. Off by default; asks once per room.
- **Slower game speed:** 90%, 80%, 70%. Marks leaderboard entries as assisted.
- **Wider hazard insets:** shrinks hazard hitboxes a little more. Marks entries as assisted.
- **Hold-to-retry** (on by default on gamepad and touch) to prevent accidental restarts.
- **Reduce motion:** softer squash and stretch, no camera shake, gentler splat particles.
- **Material outlines:** a thin high-contrast outline on every material and hazard (helps on small phone screens and for low vision).
